    Soccer: hold A with the ball to charge power, release to shoot, steer up or down to aim at a corner. Without the ball, A slides into the carrier and B switches player. Basketball: hold A to jump and let go at the top of your jump while the bar is in the green (Excellent = best odds, defenders shrink the green, step-backs get you open). Tap A to fake, press B early to cancel into a pass. Run at the rim and tap A for a layup, tap A inside the key to dunk. Without the ball, A jumps (block or rebound) and B swipes at a close ball handler.

    Career: make a player in either sport, play a 10 game season against the other teams, and spend VC on attributes and badges. Attributes and badges only affect your own player. You always control your player and teammates are CPU. Hover an attribute for what it does.

    Stamina: the bar under your player drains when you shoot, fade away, dunk or use a Move, and refills when you ease off. Below 30% you are fatigued: slower, less accurate, and a smaller green window. Below 10% you are too tired for Moves.

    Fadeaway (basketball): hold the direction away from the hoop and press A. It is harder to hit, with a tighter green window and lower odds, but defenders hurt it less. Soccer shot techniques: tap A for a toe poke, hold briefly for a placed shot, hold to full for a driven shot, hold Move while releasing for a curling finesse shot, hold B while releasing for a chip over the keeper. New techniques (hold these while releasing A): Move + B for a long outside-of-the-boot trivela curl, Move + Sprint for a wobbling knuckleball, B + Sprint for a soft dink over the keeper (a Panenka on penalties). With the ball in the air, A strikes it: a volley when low, a header when high, and a bicycle kick when it is behind you.

    Basketball 1v1 (blacktop): one half court, you check the ball at the top of the arc, no fouls or shot clock, 1s inside and 2s from deep by default, first to 11 and win by 2 (target, scoring and who gets the ball after a basket are adjustable). The ball goes back to the check after it goes out of bounds. After a miss or steal the new ball handler must take it back behind the 3-point line before shooting. Choose Streets or Indoor arena for the setting.

    Passing: hold a direction and tap B to pick the teammate in that direction (a ring marks your target). Passes to a covered teammate loft over the defender. New mechanics: in soccer hold B to charge pass power (a blue bar shows it; a quick tap is a normal pass) and foul a player in the box to give a penalty kick. In basketball hold Move and tap B near the hoop to throw an alley-oop: the receiver runs under the rim, jumps, catches it in the air and finishes. Make three shots in a row and a player is ON FIRE, with a bigger green window and better odds until the next miss. XP and unlocks: every match against the CPU earns XP, which raises your level and unlocks locker items; some items unlock from challenges such as dunks, goals or wins. Pass types: basketball: B is a chest pass, it switches to a bounce pass when a defender is in the lane (under their arms, it hits the floor about two thirds of the way) or an overhead lob over the top on long passes; hold Move and tap B for a behind-the-back or no-look pass. Soccer: B is a ground pass, a chip when the lane is blocked; hold Move and tap B for a through ball (leads the runner) or a back heel to someone behind you. A ring shows where the ball will land. Fouls: hold Sprint and press B to go for a hard foul, in basketball near the ball handler or a shooter, in soccer next to the carrier. It can draw whistles, free throws, cards and ejections, and it hurts. Green release: time the shot meter perfectly in basketball for a green flash and a bonus.

    Streets: pick Streets for either sport to play outdoors in street clothes (set your fit, cap and chain in the Locker). Dunks and layups: hold Move while pressing A near the rim for a flashy finish (windmill, tomahawk, 360, reverse, cradle, rim hang, euro step, spin, finger roll); flashy dunks can rim out when contested. Hold back plus A for a reverse. Out of bounds: soccer has throw-ins, goal kicks and corners, basketball has throw-ins. The taker is frozen, so pass with A or B (it auto-passes after a few seconds). Tournament: single elimination, eight teams. Wins and titles unlock locker items for both sports.

    Moves (Move button with the ball): no direction hesitation or stepover, up or down crossover or body feint, forward behind-the-back or rainbow flick, back step-back or drag-back, hold Sprint spin or roulette. A good move can break a defender's ankles; spins can't be stolen from. Each direction now rotates between several moves (between the legs, in-and-out, Shammgod, elastico, Cruyff turn), and a second move within about a second chains into a combo (Killer Crossover) that breaks ankles far more often; broken defenders stumble and sit down. A slide tackle that wins the ball sends the victim tumbling.

    Defense: defenders now stay goal-side, mirror the ball carrier, jump to contest shots and press harder. A hand in the face shrinks the green window and cuts the make chance, and a jumping defender hurts it most. Dunk over a defender in his way for a slow-mo poster.

    Halloween: pick a Halloween costume, spooky auras and ball effects in the locker, and play on the haunted gym, pumpkin court, graveyard pitch or pumpkin patch from Ball & arena.

    Main menu: use ↑↓ (or WS) and Enter, press 1 to 7 to jump to a tile, ←→ to swap sport, Esc to go back and F for fullscreen. Your settings, level and career are saved in this browser.

    Kicks: pick a shoe brand and model in the locker (high-tops, chunky soles, studs and brand marks), separate from shoe colour.

    Crowd: on the streets, bystanders watch from the sidelines, react to the action, heckle players and can get clipped by a stray ball. Crowd and neighbourhood ambience follows the match; the Sound button turns it all off.
